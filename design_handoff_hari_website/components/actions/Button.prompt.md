Pill-shaped action button; one `primary` per view.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" iconRight="arrow-right">Shop matcha</Button>
<Button variant="accent">Add to cart · ₹599</Button>
```

- `accent` (haldi yellow) is reserved for value/price moments — add to cart, offers.
- Sizes sm 36 / md 44 / lg 54. Icons are Lucide names.
