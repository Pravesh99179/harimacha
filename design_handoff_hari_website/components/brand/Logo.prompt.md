The Hari Matcha logo — use it for headers, footers, packaging mocks; never redraw it.

```jsx
<Logo variant="wordmark" tone="onDark" size={96} showHindi />
<Logo variant="compact" size={32} />
<Logo variant="appIcon" size={48} />
```

- `wordmark` = HAR+whisk with MATCHA tagline; `compact` = no tagline (nav); `mark` = whisk only; `appIcon` = rounded square.
- `tone="onDark"` for green grounds (cream ink), `onLight` for cream/white grounds (green ink).
