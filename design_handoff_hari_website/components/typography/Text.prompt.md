Typography primitive — every piece of copy should go through a variant.

```jsx
<Text variant="overline">Ceremonial grade</Text>
<Text variant="display-lg">Good matcha, every day.</Text>
<Text variant="body-lg" color="var(--text-secondary)">Stone-ground in Uji…</Text>
```

- display-* use Bagel Fat One (short punchy lines only); heading-* Jost 500; body Jost 400; overline = letterspaced caps from the logo.
